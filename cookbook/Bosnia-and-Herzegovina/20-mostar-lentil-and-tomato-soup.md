# Mostar Lentil And Tomato Soup

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Soup
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 55 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style soup centers on lentils with tomatoes, seasoned with paprika, garlic, and mint. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried lentils
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1.2 liters stock or water
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of paprika, garlic, and mint
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Warm the fat in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until fragrant.
2. Add the lentils, the tomatoes, and paprika, and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer gently until the main ingredient is tender.
4. Taste, crush a little of the soup to thicken it if desired, and add the seasoning blend.
5. Serve hot with bread and a final spoon of sunflower oil.

## Serving Note
Serve with crusty bread and a final drizzle of sunflower oil.
