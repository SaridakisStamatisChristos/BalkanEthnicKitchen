# Bihac Herb Flatbread

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Flatbread
- Yield: 1 loaf or tray
- Time: 1 hour 30 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style flatbread centers on dill with yogurt, seasoned with savory, butter, and sea salt. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 400 g flour or cornmeal
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp yeast or baking powder
- 250 ml warm liquid
- 180 g dill
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 2 tbsp butter or oil
- 1 tsp savory, butter, and sea salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and grease a loaf tin or tray.
2. Combine the dry ingredients, then stir in the warm liquid and fat.
3. Fold in the dill, the yogurt, and the flavoring until you have a thick batter or soft dough.
4. Spread or shape the dough in the pan.
5. Bake until the loaf is golden and sounds hollow when tapped.

## Serving Note
Serve warm from the oven or toasted the next day.
