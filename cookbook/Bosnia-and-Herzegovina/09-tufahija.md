# Tufahija

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Poached fruit dessert
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style poached fruit dessert centers on apples with walnuts, seasoned with cinnamon, cream, and syrup. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 300 g apples
- 80 g walnuts
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp cinnamon, cream, and syrup

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the apples, the walnuts, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of cinnamon, cream, and syrup come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
