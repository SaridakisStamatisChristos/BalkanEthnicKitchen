# Bosanski Lonac

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style stew centers on beef with cabbage, seasoned with root vegetables and smoked paprika. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 500 g beef
- 300 g cabbage
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of root vegetables and smoked paprika
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the beef and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the cabbage, tomatoes, and root vegetables and smoked paprika.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
