# Klepe

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Filled dough
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style filled dough centers on beef mince with garlic yogurt, seasoned with butter and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 300 g flour
- 1 egg
- 120 ml water
- 500 g beef mince
- 3 cloves garlic yogurt
- 2 tbsp melted butter or yogurt sauce
- 1 tbsp butter and parsley
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Mix the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
2. Prepare a filling from the beef mince, the garlic yogurt, and the flavoring.
3. Roll thin, cut pieces, fill, and seal carefully.
4. Boil or steam until tender, then coat with butter or yogurt sauce.
5. Serve hot.

## Serving Note
Serve hot with butter, yogurt, or pan sauce spooned over.
