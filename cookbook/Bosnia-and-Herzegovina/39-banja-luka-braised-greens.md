# Banja Luka Braised Greens

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Greens dish
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style greens dish centers on spinach with onion, seasoned with garlic, olive oil, and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g spinach
- 1 large onion
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, olive oil, and lemon
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the spinach and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the onion, tomatoes, and garlic, olive oil, and lemon.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
