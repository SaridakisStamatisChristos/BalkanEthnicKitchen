# Travnik Plum Tray Bake

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Fruit cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style fruit cake centers on plum with walnuts, seasoned with cinnamon and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 160 g plum
- 80 g walnuts
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp cinnamon and butter
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the plum, the walnuts, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
