# Herzegovina Charred Zucchini Salad

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style salad centers on zucchini with yogurt, seasoned with garlic, dill, and olive oil. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 300 g zucchini
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of garlic, dill, and olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the zucchini and the yogurt into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with sunflower oil, vinegar, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
