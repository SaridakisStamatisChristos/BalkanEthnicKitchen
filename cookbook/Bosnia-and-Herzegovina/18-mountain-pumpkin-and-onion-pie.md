# Mountain Pumpkin And Onion Pie

- Country: Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Category: Savory pie
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Bosnia and Herzegovina home-style savory pie centers on pumpkin with onion, seasoned with cinnamon, black pepper, and savory. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beef, lamb, peppers, yogurt sauces, and slow-cooked broths.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo or 500 g thin pie dough
- 3 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 300 g pumpkin
- 1 large onion
- 2 eggs
- 200 g yogurt
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of cinnamon, black pepper, and savory
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and oil a baking tray.
2. Cook the onion with the pumpkin until softened, then fold in the onion and the cinnamon, black pepper, and savory.
3. Whisk the eggs and yogurt with a pinch of salt to make a loose binding mixture.
4. Layer the dough with oil, spoon the filling between layers, and finish with the yogurt mixture over the top.
5. Bake until bronzed and crisp, then rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Serving Note
Serve warm with a spoon of yogurt and wedges of vinegar.
