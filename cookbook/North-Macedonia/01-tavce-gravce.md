# Tavce Gravce

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Baked beans
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style baked beans centers on white beans with onion, seasoned with paprika, mint, and dried pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 1 large onion
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, mint, and dried pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the white beans lightly with the onion, then add the onion and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
