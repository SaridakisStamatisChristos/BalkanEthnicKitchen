# Sutlijash

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Rice pudding
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style rice pudding centers on rice with milk, seasoned with cinnamon and vanilla. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 180 g rice
- 250 g or ml milk
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp cinnamon and vanilla

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the rice, the milk, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of cinnamon and vanilla come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
