# Kebapi

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Grilled meat
- Yield: 4 to 6 servings
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style grilled meat centers on beef mince with onion, seasoned with paprika, cumin, and garlic. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 500 g beef mince
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, cumin, and garlic
- salt and black pepper
- flatbread or raw onion for serving

## Method
1. Combine the beef mince, the onion, garlic, and the seasoning blend.
2. Mix well and let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
3. Shape into skewers, patties, or small logs depending on the dish.
4. Grill or sear over high heat until browned outside and juicy inside.
5. Serve immediately with flatbread, onion, and something cool on the side.

## Serving Note
Serve with flatbread, onion, and a cool salad or relish.
