# Tikvesh Quince Spoon Sweet

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Fruit dessert
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style fruit dessert centers on quince with sugar syrup, seasoned with cinnamon and lemon peel. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 300 g quince
- 100 g sugar syrup
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp cinnamon and lemon peel

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the quince, the sugar syrup, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of cinnamon and lemon peel come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
