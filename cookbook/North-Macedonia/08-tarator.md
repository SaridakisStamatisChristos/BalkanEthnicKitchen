# Tarator

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Chilled soup
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes plus chilling

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style chilled soup centers on cucumber with walnuts, seasoned with yogurt, garlic, and dill. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 600 g yogurt
- 250 ml cold water
- 1 large cucumber
- 80 g walnuts
- 1 tbsp seasoning mix of yogurt, garlic, and dill
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil
- salt

## Method
1. Whisk the yogurt and cold water until smooth and lightly frothy.
2. Stir in the cucumber, the walnuts, and the yogurt, garlic, and dill.
3. Season carefully with salt and a drizzle of oil.
4. Chill for at least 20 minutes so the flavors settle.
5. Serve very cold.

## Serving Note
Serve very cold on a hot day or beside grilled meat.
