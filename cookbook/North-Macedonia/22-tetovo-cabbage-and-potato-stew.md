# Tetovo Cabbage And Potato Stew

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style stew centers on cabbage with potatoes, seasoned with paprika, onion, and savory. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g cabbage
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, onion, and savory
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the cabbage and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the potatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, onion, and savory.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
