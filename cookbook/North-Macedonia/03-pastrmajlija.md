# Pastrmajlija

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Open pie
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style open pie centers on bread dough with beef or lamb, seasoned with paprika and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo or 500 g thin pie dough
- 3 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 500 g bread dough
- 500 g beef or lamb
- 2 eggs
- 200 g yogurt
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and oil a baking tray.
2. Cook the onion with the bread dough until softened, then fold in the beef or lamb and the paprika and black pepper.
3. Whisk the eggs and yogurt with a pinch of salt to make a loose binding mixture.
4. Layer the dough with oil, spoon the filling between layers, and finish with the yogurt mixture over the top.
5. Bake until bronzed and crisp, then rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Serving Note
Serve warm with a spoon of yogurt and wedges of vinegar.
