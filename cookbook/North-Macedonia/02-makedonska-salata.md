# Makedonska Salata

- Country: North Macedonia
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original North Macedonia home-style salad centers on tomatoes with cucumber, seasoned with roasted pepper, onion, and sirene cheese. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, peppers, yogurt, grilled meats, and bright paprika.

## Ingredients
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 large cucumber
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of roasted pepper, onion, and sirene cheese
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the tomatoes and the cucumber into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with sunflower oil, vinegar, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
