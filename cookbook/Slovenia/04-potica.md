# Potica

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Rolled pastry
- Yield: 8 to 10 slices
- Time: 2 hours

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style rolled pastry centers on walnuts with honey, seasoned with sweet dough and cinnamon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 350 g flour
- 7 g yeast
- 150 ml warm milk or water
- 60 g butter
- 80 g walnuts
- 120 g honey
- 1 egg for brushing
- 1 tbsp sweet dough and cinnamon

## Method
1. Prepare the enriched dough and let it rise once.
2. Spread the filling made from the walnuts, the honey, and the flavoring across the dough.
3. Roll tightly, place in the pan, and let it proof again.
4. Bake until golden and fragrant.
5. Cool enough to slice cleanly.

## Serving Note
Slice after a short cooling period so the swirl holds.
