# Matevz

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Bean mash
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style bean mash centers on beans with potatoes, seasoned with garlic, cracklings, and pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 220 g cornmeal, flour, or cooked beans
- 700 ml water or milk
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried beans
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp garlic, cracklings, and pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a simmer in a heavy pot.
2. Whisk in the meal or bean base so it thickens without lumps.
3. Fold in the beans, the potatoes, and the flavoring.
4. Cook on low heat until the porridge is thick, silky, and rich.
5. Serve hot with extra butter, cheese, or yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve hot with extra cheese, yogurt, or butter on top.
