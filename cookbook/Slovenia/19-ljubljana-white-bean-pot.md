# Ljubljana White Bean Pot

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Bean stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style bean stew centers on white beans with tomatoes, seasoned with paprika, garlic, and chives. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seed oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 400 g tomatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, garlic, and chives
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the white beans and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, garlic, and chives.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
