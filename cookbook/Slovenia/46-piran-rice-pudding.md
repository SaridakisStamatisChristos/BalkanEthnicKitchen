# Piran Rice Pudding

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Pudding
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style pudding centers on rice with milk, seasoned with vanilla and cinnamon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 180 g rice
- 250 g or ml milk
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp vanilla and cinnamon

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the rice, the milk, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of vanilla and cinnamon come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
