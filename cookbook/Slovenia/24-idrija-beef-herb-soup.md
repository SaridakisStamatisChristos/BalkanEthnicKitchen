# Idrija Beef Herb Soup

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Soup
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 55 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style soup centers on beef with onion, seasoned with parsley, garlic, and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seed oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 500 g beef
- 1 large onion
- 1.2 liters stock or water
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of parsley, garlic, and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Warm the fat in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until fragrant.
2. Add the beef, the onion, and paprika, and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer gently until the main ingredient is tender.
4. Taste, crush a little of the soup to thicken it if desired, and add the seasoning blend.
5. Serve hot with bread and a final spoon of pumpkin seed oil.

## Serving Note
Serve with crusty bread and a final drizzle of pumpkin seed oil.
