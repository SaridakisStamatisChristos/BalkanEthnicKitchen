# Idrijski Zlikrofi

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Filled dough
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style filled dough centers on potatoes with onion, seasoned with chives and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 300 g flour
- 1 egg
- 120 ml water
- 400 g potatoes
- 1 large onion
- 2 tbsp melted butter or yogurt sauce
- 1 tbsp chives and butter
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Mix the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
2. Prepare a filling from the potatoes, the onion, and the flavoring.
3. Roll thin, cut pieces, fill, and seal carefully.
4. Boil or steam until tender, then coat with butter or yogurt sauce.
5. Serve hot.

## Serving Note
Serve hot with butter, yogurt, or pan sauce spooned over.
