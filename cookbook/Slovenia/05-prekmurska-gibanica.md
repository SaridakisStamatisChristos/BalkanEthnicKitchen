# Prekmurska Gibanica

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Layered pastry
- Yield: 10 pieces
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style layered pastry centers on apples with poppy seeds, seasoned with walnuts, quark, and raisins. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo dough
- 80 g melted butter
- 300 g apples
- 80 g poppy seeds
- 120 ml syrup or honey
- 1 tbsp walnuts, quark, and raisins

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C and butter the baking tin.
2. Prepare the filling from the apples, the poppy seeds, and the flavoring.
3. Layer phyllo with butter and the filling, cutting the pastry before it bakes if you want clean portions.
4. Bake until crisp and deeply golden.
5. Finish with syrup or honey while still warm if the pastry is meant to be sweet.

## Serving Note
Let the pastry settle slightly before cutting neat portions.
