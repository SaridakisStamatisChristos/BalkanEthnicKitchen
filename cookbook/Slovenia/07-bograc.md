# Bograc

- Country: Slovenia
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Slovenia home-style stew centers on beef and pork with potatoes, seasoned with paprika, wine, and garlic. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, sauerkraut, buckwheat, dairy, and alpine pastry traditions.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seed oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 500 g beef and pork
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, wine, and garlic
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the beef and pork and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the potatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, wine, and garlic.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
