# Menemen

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Skillet main
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style skillet main centers on eggs with peppers, seasoned with tomato, olive oil, and oregano. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 large eggs
- 6 medium peppers
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of tomato, olive oil, and oregano
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the fat in a wide pan and soften the onion.
2. Add the eggs and the peppers, and cook until the mixture starts to concentrate.
3. Season with tomato, olive oil, and oregano and cook until the pan looks glossy and thick.
4. If eggs or cheese belong in the dish, fold them through right at the end.
5. Serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Serving Note
Bring the whole pan to the table with bread for scooping.
