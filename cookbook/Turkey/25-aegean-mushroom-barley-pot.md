# Aegean Mushroom Barley Pot

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style stew centers on mushrooms with barley, seasoned with garlic, onion, and oregano. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g mushrooms
- 180 g barley
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, onion, and oregano
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the barley, tomatoes, and garlic, onion, and oregano.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
