# Kisir

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style salad centers on bulgur with tomatoes, seasoned with parsley, pomegranate molasses, and mint. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 180 g bulgur
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of parsley, pomegranate molasses, and mint
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the bulgur and the tomatoes into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
