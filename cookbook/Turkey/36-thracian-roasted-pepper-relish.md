# Thracian Roasted Pepper Relish

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Relish
- Yield: 3 cups
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style relish centers on peppers with tomatoes, seasoned with garlic, vinegar, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 6 medium peppers
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, vinegar, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Roast or soften the peppers and the tomatoes until sweet and tender.
2. Cook with onion, garlic, oil, and acid until the mixture thickens.
3. Blend or mash to the texture you want, then season with garlic, vinegar, and parsley.
4. Simmer briefly again to concentrate the flavor.
5. Cool and serve as a spread or side dish.

## Serving Note
Serve at room temperature as a spread or side dish.
