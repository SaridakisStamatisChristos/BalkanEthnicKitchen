# Balik Ekmek

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Street sandwich
- Yield: 4 sandwiches
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style street sandwich centers on grilled fish with bread, seasoned with onion, greens, and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 600 g grilled fish
- 4 pieces bread
- 4 bread rolls or half loaves
- 1 lemon, cut in wedges
- handful of fresh greens
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp onion, greens, and lemon

## Method
1. Cook or grill the grilled fish and season it with onion, greens, and lemon.
2. Warm or toast the bread.
3. Pile the bread with the main filling, the bread, and the greens.
4. Finish with lemon and a little oil.
5. Serve immediately while the bread is still crisp.

## Serving Note
Eat straight away while the bread and filling are both warm.
