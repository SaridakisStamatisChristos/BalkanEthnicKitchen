# Aegean Charred Zucchini Salad

- Country: Turkey
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Turkey home-style salad centers on zucchini with yogurt, seasoned with garlic, dill, and olive oil. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, peppers, bulgur, yogurt, grilled meats, and syrup sweets.

## Ingredients
- 300 g zucchini
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of garlic, dill, and olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the zucchini and the yogurt into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
