# Crete Okra And Potato Stew

- Country: Greece
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style stew centers on okra with potatoes, seasoned with paprika, onion, and mint. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g okra
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, onion, and mint
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the okra and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the potatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, onion, and mint.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
