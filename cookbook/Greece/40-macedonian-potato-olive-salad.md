# Macedonian Potato Olive Salad

- Country: Greece
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style salad centers on potatoes with olives, seasoned with parsley, onion, and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 400 g potatoes
- 200 g olives
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of parsley, onion, and lemon
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the potatoes and the olives into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
