# Athens Charcoal Goat Skewers

- Country: Greece
- Category: Grilled meat
- Yield: 4 to 6 servings
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style grilled meat centers on goat with onion, seasoned with garlic, paprika, and oregano. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 600 g goat
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, paprika, and oregano
- salt and black pepper
- flatbread or raw onion for serving

## Method
1. Combine the goat, the onion, garlic, and the seasoning blend.
2. Mix well and let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
3. Shape into skewers, patties, or small logs depending on the dish.
4. Grill or sear over high heat until browned outside and juicy inside.
5. Serve immediately with flatbread, onion, and something cool on the side.

## Serving Note
Serve with flatbread, onion, and a cool salad or relish.
