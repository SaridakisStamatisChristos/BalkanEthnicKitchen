# Athens Tomato Cucumber Salad

- Country: Greece
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style salad centers on tomatoes with cucumber, seasoned with onion, oregano, and feta. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 large cucumber
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of onion, oregano, and feta
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the tomatoes and the cucumber into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
