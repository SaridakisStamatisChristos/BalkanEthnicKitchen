# Gemista

- Country: Greece
- Category: Stuffed main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style stuffed main centers on tomatoes and peppers with rice, seasoned with herbs, olive oil, and pine nuts. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 8 medium mixed tomatoes and peppers
- 180 g rice
- 250 g minced meat or chopped mushrooms
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, grated
- 500 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of herbs, olive oil, and pine nuts
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Prepare the vegetables or leaves so they are ready to fill.
2. Cook the onion with the rice and mix in the minced meat or mushrooms, then season with herbs, olive oil, and pine nuts.
3. Fill the tomatoes and peppers loosely and arrange them snugly in a pot or tray.
4. Add grated tomato, stock, and oil, then cover and cook until the filling is tender.
5. Rest briefly before serving so the parcels hold together.

## Serving Note
Serve with pan juices and a spoon of yogurt or sour cream.
