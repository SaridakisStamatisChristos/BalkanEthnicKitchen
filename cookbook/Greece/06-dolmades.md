# Dolmades

- Country: Greece
- Category: Stuffed main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style stuffed main centers on vine leaves with rice, seasoned with lemon, dill, and onion. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 16 prepared vine leaves
- 180 g rice
- 250 g minced meat or chopped mushrooms
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, grated
- 500 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of lemon, dill, and onion
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Prepare the vegetables or leaves so they are ready to fill.
2. Cook the onion with the rice and mix in the minced meat or mushrooms, then season with lemon, dill, and onion.
3. Fill the vine leaves loosely and arrange them snugly in a pot or tray.
4. Add grated tomato, stock, and oil, then cover and cook until the filling is tender.
5. Rest briefly before serving so the parcels hold together.

## Serving Note
Serve with pan juices and a spoon of yogurt or sour cream.
