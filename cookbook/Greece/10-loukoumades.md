# Loukoumades

- Country: Greece
- Category: Fried sweet
- Yield: 16 to 20 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Greece home-style fried sweet centers on honey with walnuts, seasoned with cinnamon and sugar. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, lemon, oregano, feta, vegetables, and island-style sweets.

## Ingredients
- 350 g flour
- 1 tsp yeast or baking powder
- 200 ml warm liquid
- 150 g honey
- 80 g walnuts
- oil for frying
- 1 tsp cinnamon and sugar
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Mix the dough ingredients into a soft, slightly sticky dough or batter.
2. Fold in the honey, the walnuts, and the flavoring, then rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Heat a few centimeters of oil in a pan.
4. Drop or shape portions into the oil and fry until puffed and deeply golden.
5. Drain well and finish with the sweet or savory garnish that suits the dish.

## Serving Note
Best eaten warm, either dusted with sugar or paired with cheese.
