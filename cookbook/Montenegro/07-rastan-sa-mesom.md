# Rastan sa Mesom

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style stew centers on collard greens with smoked meat, seasoned with garlic, potato, and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g collard greens
- 500 g smoked meat
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, potato, and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the collard greens and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the smoked meat, tomatoes, and garlic, potato, and black pepper.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
