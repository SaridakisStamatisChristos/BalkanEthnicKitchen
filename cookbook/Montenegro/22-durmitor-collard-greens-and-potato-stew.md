# Durmitor Collard Greens And Potato Stew

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style stew centers on collard greens with potatoes, seasoned with paprika, onion, and rosemary. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g collard greens
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, onion, and rosemary
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the collard greens and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the potatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, onion, and rosemary.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
