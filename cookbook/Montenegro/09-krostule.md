# Krostule

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Crisp pastry
- Yield: 16 to 20 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style crisp pastry centers on eggs with sugar, seasoned with citrus zest and vanilla. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 350 g flour
- 1 tsp yeast or baking powder
- 200 ml warm liquid
- 4 large eggs
- 100 g sugar
- oil for frying
- 1 tsp citrus zest and vanilla
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Mix the dough ingredients into a soft, slightly sticky dough or batter.
2. Fold in the eggs, the sugar, and the flavoring, then rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Heat a few centimeters of oil in a pan.
4. Drop or shape portions into the oil and fry until puffed and deeply golden.
5. Drain well and finish with the sweet or savory garnish that suits the dish.

## Serving Note
Best eaten warm, either dusted with sugar or paired with cheese.
