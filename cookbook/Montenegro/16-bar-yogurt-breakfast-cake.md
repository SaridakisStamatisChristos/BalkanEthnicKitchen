# Bar Yogurt Breakfast Cake

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Breakfast cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style breakfast cake centers on yogurt with semolina, seasoned with vanilla and citrus zest. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 250 g semolina
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp vanilla and citrus zest
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the yogurt, the semolina, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
