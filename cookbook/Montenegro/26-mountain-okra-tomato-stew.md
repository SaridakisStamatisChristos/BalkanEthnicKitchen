# Mountain Okra Tomato Stew

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Vegetable stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style vegetable stew centers on okra with tomatoes, seasoned with garlic, parsley, and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g okra
- 400 g tomatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, parsley, and lemon
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the okra and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomatoes, and garlic, parsley, and lemon.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
