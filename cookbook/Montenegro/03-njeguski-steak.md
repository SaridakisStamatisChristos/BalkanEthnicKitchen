# Njeguski Steak

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Baked main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style baked main centers on pork or veal with ham and cheese, seasoned with mustard, cream, and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g pork or veal
- 200 g ham and cheese
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of mustard, cream, and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the pork or veal lightly with the onion, then add the ham and cheese and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
