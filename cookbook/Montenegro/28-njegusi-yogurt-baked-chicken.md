# Njegusi Yogurt-Baked Chicken

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Baked main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style baked main centers on chicken with yogurt, seasoned with garlic, paprika, and mint. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g chicken
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, paprika, and mint
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the chicken lightly with the onion, then add the yogurt and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
