# Kotor Chicken Rice Broth

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Soup
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 55 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style soup centers on chicken with rice, seasoned with root vegetables and dill. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 500 g chicken
- 180 g rice
- 1.2 liters stock or water
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of root vegetables and dill
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Warm the fat in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken, the rice, and paprika, and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer gently until the main ingredient is tender.
4. Taste, crush a little of the soup to thicken it if desired, and add the seasoning blend.
5. Serve hot with bread and a final spoon of olive oil.

## Serving Note
Serve with crusty bread and a final drizzle of olive oil.
