# Mountain Pickled Cabbage Slaw

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style salad centers on cabbage with carrot, seasoned with vinegar, pepper, and rosemary. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 300 g cabbage
- 200 g carrot
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of vinegar, pepper, and rosemary
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the cabbage and the carrot into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
