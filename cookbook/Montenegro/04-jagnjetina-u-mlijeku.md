# Jagnjetina u Mlijeku

- Country: Montenegro
- Category: Braised main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Montenegro home-style braised main centers on lamb with milk, seasoned with garlic, carrots, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of mountain dairy, lamb, smoked meat, cornmeal, and coastal frying.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 500 g lamb
- 250 g or ml milk
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, carrots, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the lamb and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the milk, tomatoes, and garlic, carrots, and parsley.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
