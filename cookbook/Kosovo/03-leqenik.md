# Leqenik

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Corn bread
- Yield: 1 loaf or tray
- Time: 1 hour 30 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style corn bread centers on leeks with cornmeal, seasoned with yogurt and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 400 g flour or cornmeal
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp yeast or baking powder
- 250 ml warm liquid
- 300 g leeks
- 250 g cornmeal
- 2 tbsp butter or oil
- 1 tsp yogurt and butter

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and grease a loaf tin or tray.
2. Combine the dry ingredients, then stir in the warm liquid and fat.
3. Fold in the leeks, the cornmeal, and the flavoring until you have a thick batter or soft dough.
4. Spread or shape the dough in the pan.
5. Bake until the loaf is golden and sounds hollow when tapped.

## Serving Note
Serve warm from the oven or toasted the next day.
