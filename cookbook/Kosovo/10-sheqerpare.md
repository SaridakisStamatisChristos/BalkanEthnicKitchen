# Sheqerpare

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Syrup cookie
- Yield: 24 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style syrup cookie centers on semolina with butter, seasoned with sugar syrup and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 280 g flour
- 120 g butter
- 100 g sugar
- 250 g semolina
- 80 g butter
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp sugar syrup and lemon
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line a tray.
2. Cream the butter and sugar, then add the egg.
3. Fold in the flour with the semolina, the butter, and the flavoring.
4. Shape into small portions and bake until just golden at the edges.
5. Cool before storing or finishing with syrup or jam.

## Serving Note
Store in a tin and serve with coffee or tea.
