# Tav Prizreni

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Baked main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style baked main centers on lamb with peppers, seasoned with tomato, onion, and garlic. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 500 g lamb
- 6 medium peppers
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of tomato, onion, and garlic
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the lamb lightly with the onion, then add the peppers and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
