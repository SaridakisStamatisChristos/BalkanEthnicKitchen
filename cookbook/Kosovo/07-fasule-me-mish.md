# Fasule me Mish

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Bean stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style bean stew centers on white beans with beef, seasoned with paprika, garlic, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 500 g beef
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, garlic, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the white beans and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the beef, tomatoes, and paprika, garlic, and parsley.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
