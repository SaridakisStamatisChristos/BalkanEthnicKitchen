# Dukagjin Cherry Spoon Sweet

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Fruit dessert
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style fruit dessert centers on cherry with sugar syrup, seasoned with cinnamon and lemon peel. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 300 g cherry
- 100 g sugar syrup
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp cinnamon and lemon peel

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the cherry, the sugar syrup, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of cinnamon and lemon peel come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
