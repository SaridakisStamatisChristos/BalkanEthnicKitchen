# Flija

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Layered bake
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style layered bake centers on cream with butter, seasoned with yogurt and layered batter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 250 g or ml cream
- 200 g butter
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of yogurt and layered batter
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the cream lightly with the onion, then add the butter and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
