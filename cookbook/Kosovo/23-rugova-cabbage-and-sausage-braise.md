# Rugova Cabbage And Sausage Braise

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style stew centers on cabbage with sausage, seasoned with garlic, black pepper, and paprika. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 300 g cabbage
- 500 g sausage
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, black pepper, and paprika
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the cabbage and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the sausage, tomatoes, and garlic, black pepper, and paprika.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
