# Peja Semolina Syrup Cake

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Syrup cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style syrup cake centers on semolina with yogurt, seasoned with lemon syrup and vanilla. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 250 g semolina
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp lemon syrup and vanilla
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the semolina, the yogurt, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
