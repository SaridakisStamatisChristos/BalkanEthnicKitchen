# Pristina Fig Tray Bake

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Fruit cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style fruit cake centers on fig with walnuts, seasoned with cinnamon and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 300 g fig
- 80 g walnuts
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp cinnamon and butter
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the fig, the walnuts, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
