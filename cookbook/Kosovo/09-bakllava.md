# Bakllava

- Country: Kosovo
- Category: Layered pastry
- Yield: 10 pieces
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Kosovo home-style layered pastry centers on walnuts with butter, seasoned with honey and cinnamon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of lamb, dairy, peppers, layered breads, and family-style tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo dough
- 80 g melted butter
- 80 g walnuts
- 120 g butter
- 120 ml syrup or honey
- 1 tbsp honey and cinnamon

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C and butter the baking tin.
2. Prepare the filling from the walnuts, the butter, and the flavoring.
3. Layer phyllo with butter and the filling, cutting the pastry before it bakes if you want clean portions.
4. Bake until crisp and deeply golden.
5. Finish with syrup or honey while still warm if the pastry is meant to be sweet.

## Serving Note
Let the pastry settle slightly before cutting neat portions.
