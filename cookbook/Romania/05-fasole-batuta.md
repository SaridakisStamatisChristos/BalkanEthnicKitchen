# Fasole Batuta

- Country: Romania
- Category: Bean dip
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style bean dip centers on white beans with garlic, seasoned with caramelized onion and paprika oil. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 2 to 4 tbsp yogurt or water as needed
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of caramelized onion and paprika oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Blend or mash the white beans with the garlic, oil, and a splash of acid.
2. Thin with yogurt or water until spreadable.
3. Fold in the seasoning blend of caramelized onion and paprika oil.
4. Taste for salt and chill briefly if you want a firmer texture.
5. Serve with bread, vegetables, or grilled meat.

## Serving Note
Serve chilled or cool with bread and raw vegetables.
