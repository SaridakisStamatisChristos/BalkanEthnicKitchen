# Zacusca

- Country: Romania
- Category: Relish
- Yield: 3 cups
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Romania home-style relish centers on eggplant with roasted peppers, seasoned with tomato, onion, and bay leaf. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 300 g eggplant
- 6 medium roasted peppers
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of tomato, onion, and bay leaf
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Roast or soften the eggplant and the roasted peppers until sweet and tender.
2. Cook with onion, garlic, oil, and acid until the mixture thickens.
3. Blend or mash to the texture you want, then season with tomato, onion, and bay leaf.
4. Simmer briefly again to concentrate the flavor.
5. Cool and serve as a spread or side dish.

## Serving Note
Serve at room temperature as a spread or side dish.
