# Maramures Beef Herb Soup

- Country: Romania
- Category: Soup
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 55 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style soup centers on beef with onion, seasoned with dill, garlic, and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 500 g beef
- 1 large onion
- 1.2 liters stock or water
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of dill, garlic, and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Warm the fat in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until fragrant.
2. Add the beef, the onion, and paprika, and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer gently until the main ingredient is tender.
4. Taste, crush a little of the soup to thicken it if desired, and add the seasoning blend.
5. Serve hot with bread and a final spoon of sunflower oil.

## Serving Note
Serve with crusty bread and a final drizzle of sunflower oil.
