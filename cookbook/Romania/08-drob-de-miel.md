# Drob de Miel

- Country: Romania
- Category: Baked main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style baked main centers on lamb offal with greens, seasoned with eggs, dill, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 500 g lamb offal
- 300 g greens
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of eggs, dill, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the lamb offal lightly with the onion, then add the greens and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
