# Mamaliga cu Branza

- Country: Romania
- Category: Porridge
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style porridge centers on brined cheese with sour cream, seasoned with cornmeal and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 220 g cornmeal, flour, or cooked beans
- 700 ml water or milk
- 180 g brined cheese
- 250 g or ml sour cream
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp cornmeal and butter
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a simmer in a heavy pot.
2. Whisk in the meal or bean base so it thickens without lumps.
3. Fold in the brined cheese, the sour cream, and the flavoring.
4. Cook on low heat until the porridge is thick, silky, and rich.
5. Serve hot with extra butter, cheese, or yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve hot with extra cheese, yogurt, or butter on top.
