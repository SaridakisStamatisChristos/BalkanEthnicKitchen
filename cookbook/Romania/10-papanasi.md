# Papanasi

- Country: Romania
- Category: Fried dessert
- Yield: 16 to 20 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style fried dessert centers on fresh cheese with berry jam, seasoned with sour cream and vanilla. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 350 g flour
- 1 tsp yeast or baking powder
- 200 ml warm liquid
- 150 g fresh cheese
- 80 g berry jam
- oil for frying
- 1 tsp sour cream and vanilla
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Mix the dough ingredients into a soft, slightly sticky dough or batter.
2. Fold in the fresh cheese, the berry jam, and the flavoring, then rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Heat a few centimeters of oil in a pan.
4. Drop or shape portions into the oil and fry until puffed and deeply golden.
5. Drain well and finish with the sweet or savory garnish that suits the dish.

## Serving Note
Best eaten warm, either dusted with sugar or paired with cheese.
