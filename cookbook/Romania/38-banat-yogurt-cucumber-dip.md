# Banat Yogurt Cucumber Dip

- Country: Romania
- Category: Dip
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Romania home-style dip centers on cucumber with yogurt, seasoned with garlic, thyme, and olive oil. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sour soups, cornmeal, roasted peppers, pork, and nut-rich baking.

## Ingredients
- 1 large cucumber
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 2 to 4 tbsp yogurt or water as needed
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, thyme, and olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Blend or mash the cucumber with the yogurt, oil, and a splash of acid.
2. Thin with yogurt or water until spreadable.
3. Fold in the seasoning blend of garlic, thyme, and olive oil.
4. Taste for salt and chill briefly if you want a firmer texture.
5. Serve with bread, vegetables, or grilled meat.

## Serving Note
Serve chilled or cool with bread and raw vegetables.
