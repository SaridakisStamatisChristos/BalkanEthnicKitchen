# Vojvodina Onions And Potato Stew

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style stew centers on onions with potatoes, seasoned with paprika, onion, and savory. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 1 large onions
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, onion, and savory
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the onions and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the potatoes, tomatoes, and paprika, onion, and savory.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
