# Village Pickled Cabbage Slaw

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style salad centers on cabbage with carrot, seasoned with vinegar, pepper, and savory. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 300 g cabbage
- 200 g carrot
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of vinegar, pepper, and savory
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the cabbage and the carrot into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with sunflower oil, vinegar, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
