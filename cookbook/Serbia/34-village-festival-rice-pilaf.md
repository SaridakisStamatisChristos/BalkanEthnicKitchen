# Village Festival Rice Pilaf

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Rice main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style rice main centers on rice with onion, seasoned with butter, broth, and savory. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 180 g rice
- 1 large onion
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of butter, broth, and savory
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the rice lightly with the onion, then add the onion and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
