# Vanilice

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Sandwich cookie
- Yield: 24 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style sandwich cookie centers on butter with jam, seasoned with vanilla sugar and lemon zest. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 280 g flour
- 120 g butter
- 100 g sugar
- 120 g butter
- 80 g jam
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla sugar and lemon zest
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line a tray.
2. Cream the butter and sugar, then add the egg.
3. Fold in the flour with the butter, the jam, and the flavoring.
4. Shape into small portions and bake until just golden at the edges.
5. Cool before storing or finishing with syrup or jam.

## Serving Note
Store in a tin and serve with coffee or tea.
