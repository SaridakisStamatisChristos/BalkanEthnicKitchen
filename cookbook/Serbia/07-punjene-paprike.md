# Punjene Paprike

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Stuffed main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style stuffed main centers on sweet peppers with rice, seasoned with beef, tomato, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 8 medium sweet peppers
- 180 g rice
- 250 g minced meat or chopped mushrooms
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, grated
- 500 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of beef, tomato, and parsley
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Prepare the vegetables or leaves so they are ready to fill.
2. Cook the onion with the rice and mix in the minced meat or mushrooms, then season with beef, tomato, and parsley.
3. Fill the sweet peppers loosely and arrange them snugly in a pot or tray.
4. Add grated tomato, stock, and oil, then cover and cook until the filling is tender.
5. Rest briefly before serving so the parcels hold together.

## Serving Note
Serve with pan juices and a spoon of yogurt or sour cream.
