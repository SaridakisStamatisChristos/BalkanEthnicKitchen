# Sumadija Leek Coil Pie

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Savory pie
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style savory pie centers on leek with white cheese, seasoned with dill and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo or 500 g thin pie dough
- 3 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 300 g leek
- 180 g white cheese
- 2 eggs
- 200 g yogurt
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of dill and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and oil a baking tray.
2. Cook the onion with the leek until softened, then fold in the white cheese and the dill and black pepper.
3. Whisk the eggs and yogurt with a pinch of salt to make a loose binding mixture.
4. Layer the dough with oil, spoon the filling between layers, and finish with the yogurt mixture over the top.
5. Bake until bronzed and crisp, then rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Serving Note
Serve warm with a spoon of yogurt and wedges of vinegar.
