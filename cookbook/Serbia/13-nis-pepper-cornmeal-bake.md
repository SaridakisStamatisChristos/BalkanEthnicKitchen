# Nis Pepper Cornmeal Bake

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Savory bake
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style savory bake centers on peppers with cornmeal, seasoned with garlic, yogurt, and mint. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 6 medium peppers
- 250 g cornmeal
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, yogurt, and mint
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the peppers lightly with the onion, then add the cornmeal and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
