# Kopaonik Yogurt Honey Cream

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Cream dessert
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style cream dessert centers on yogurt with honey, seasoned with vanilla and walnut. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 250 g or ml yogurt
- 100 g honey
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp vanilla and walnut

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the yogurt, the honey, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of vanilla and walnut come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
