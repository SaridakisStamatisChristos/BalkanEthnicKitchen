# Nis Garlic Bean Mash

- Country: Serbia
- Category: Bean dip
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Serbia home-style bean dip centers on white beans with garlic, seasoned with paprika oil and lemon. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of beans, grilled meat, paprika, corn breads, and robust tray bakes.

## Ingredients
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 2 to 4 tbsp yogurt or water as needed
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika oil and lemon
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Blend or mash the white beans with the garlic, oil, and a splash of acid.
2. Thin with yogurt or water until spreadable.
3. Fold in the seasoning blend of paprika oil and lemon.
4. Taste for salt and chill briefly if you want a firmer texture.
5. Serve with bread, vegetables, or grilled meat.

## Serving Note
Serve chilled or cool with bread and raw vegetables.
