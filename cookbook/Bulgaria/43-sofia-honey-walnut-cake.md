# Sofia Honey Walnut Cake

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style cake centers on honey with walnut, seasoned with vanilla and citrus zest. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 160 g honey
- 120 g walnut
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp vanilla and citrus zest
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the honey, the walnut, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
