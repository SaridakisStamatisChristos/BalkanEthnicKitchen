# Bob Chorba

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Soup
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 55 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style soup centers on white beans with carrot, seasoned with paprika, mint, and tomato. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 300 g cooked or 180 g dried white beans
- 160 g carrot
- 1.2 liters stock or water
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of paprika, mint, and tomato
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Warm the fat in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until fragrant.
2. Add the white beans, the carrot, and paprika, and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer gently until the main ingredient is tender.
4. Taste, crush a little of the soup to thicken it if desired, and add the seasoning blend.
5. Serve hot with bread and a final spoon of sunflower oil.

## Serving Note
Serve with crusty bread and a final drizzle of sunflower oil.
