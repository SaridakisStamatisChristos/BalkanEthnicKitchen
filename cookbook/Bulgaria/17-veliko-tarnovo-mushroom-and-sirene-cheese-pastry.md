# Veliko Tarnovo Mushroom And Sirene Cheese Pastry

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Savory pastry
- Yield: 10 pieces
- Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style savory pastry centers on mushrooms with sirene cheese, seasoned with parsley and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo dough
- 80 g melted butter
- 300 g mushrooms
- 120 g sirene cheese
- 120 ml syrup or honey
- 1 tbsp parsley and butter

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C and butter the baking tin.
2. Prepare the filling from the mushrooms, the sirene cheese, and the flavoring.
3. Layer phyllo with butter and the filling, cutting the pastry before it bakes if you want clean portions.
4. Bake until crisp and deeply golden.
5. Finish with syrup or honey while still warm if the pastry is meant to be sweet.

## Serving Note
Let the pastry settle slightly before cutting neat portions.
