# Veliko Tarnovo Eggplant Casserole

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Vegetable bake
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style vegetable bake centers on eggplant with tomatoes, seasoned with garlic, onion, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 300 g eggplant
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, onion, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the eggplant lightly with the onion, then add the tomatoes and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
