# Kozunak

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Sweet bread
- Yield: 1 loaf
- Time: 2 hours 30 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style sweet bread centers on walnuts with milk, seasoned with eggs, vanilla, and citrus zest. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 450 g flour
- 7 g yeast
- 120 ml warm liquid
- 80 g sugar
- 2 eggs
- 80 g butter
- 80 g walnuts
- 250 g or ml milk
- 1 tbsp eggs, vanilla, and citrus zest

## Method
1. Mix the dough and knead until smooth, then let it rise until doubled.
2. Work in the walnuts, the milk, and the flavoring.
3. Shape into a braid or loaf and let it rise again.
4. Brush with egg and bake until deeply golden.
5. Cool before slicing so the crumb sets properly.

## Serving Note
Slice once cool and serve plain or with extra jam.
