# Strandzha Potato Olive Salad

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style salad centers on potatoes with olives, seasoned with parsley, onion, and vinegar. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 400 g potatoes
- 200 g olives
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp vinegar juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of parsley, onion, and vinegar
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the potatoes and the olives into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with sunflower oil, vinegar, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
