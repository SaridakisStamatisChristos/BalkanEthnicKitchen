# Mish-Mash

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Skillet main
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style skillet main centers on eggs with peppers, seasoned with tomato, sirene cheese, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 4 large eggs
- 6 medium peppers
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of tomato, sirene cheese, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the fat in a wide pan and soften the onion.
2. Add the eggs and the peppers, and cook until the mixture starts to concentrate.
3. Season with tomato, sirene cheese, and parsley and cook until the pan looks glossy and thick.
4. If eggs or cheese belong in the dish, fold them through right at the end.
5. Serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Serving Note
Bring the whole pan to the table with bread for scooping.
