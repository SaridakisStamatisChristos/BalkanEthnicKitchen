# Kebapche

- Country: Bulgaria
- Category: Grilled meat
- Yield: 4 to 6 servings
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Bulgaria home-style grilled meat centers on minced pork and beef with onion, seasoned with cumin, black pepper, and paprika. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of sirene cheese, peppers, yogurt, mint, and hearty clay pot dishes.

## Ingredients
- 500 g minced pork and beef
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of cumin, black pepper, and paprika
- salt and black pepper
- flatbread or raw onion for serving

## Method
1. Combine the minced pork and beef, the onion, garlic, and the seasoning blend.
2. Mix well and let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
3. Shape into skewers, patties, or small logs depending on the dish.
4. Grill or sear over high heat until browned outside and juicy inside.
5. Serve immediately with flatbread, onion, and something cool on the side.

## Serving Note
Serve with flatbread, onion, and a cool salad or relish.
