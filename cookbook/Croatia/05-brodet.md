# Brodet

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Fish stew
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style fish stew centers on mixed fish with tomatoes, seasoned with garlic, wine, and bay leaf. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 600 g mixed fish
- 400 g tomatoes
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 700 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, wine, and bay leaf
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Brown the onion in fat until golden.
2. Add the mixed fish and cook until it picks up some color.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomatoes, and garlic, wine, and bay leaf.
4. Add the stock, cover, and simmer slowly until rich and tender.
5. Adjust salt, reduce the sauce if needed, and serve with bread or a spoon of yogurt.

## Serving Note
Serve with bread, polenta, or a sharp salad.
