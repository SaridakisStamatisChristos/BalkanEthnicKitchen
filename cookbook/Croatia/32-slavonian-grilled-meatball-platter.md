# Slavonian Grilled Meatball Platter

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Grilled meat
- Yield: 4 to 6 servings
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style grilled meat centers on sausage with onion, seasoned with paprika, garlic, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 500 g sausage
- 1 large onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of paprika, garlic, and parsley
- salt and black pepper
- flatbread or raw onion for serving

## Method
1. Combine the sausage, the onion, garlic, and the seasoning blend.
2. Mix well and let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
3. Shape into skewers, patties, or small logs depending on the dish.
4. Grill or sear over high heat until browned outside and juicy inside.
5. Serve immediately with flatbread, onion, and something cool on the side.

## Serving Note
Serve with flatbread, onion, and a cool salad or relish.
