# Zadar Sesame Cookies

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Cookie
- Yield: 24 pieces
- Time: 45 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style cookie centers on sesame seeds with butter, seasoned with orange zest and sugar. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 280 g flour
- 120 g butter
- 100 g sugar
- 120 g sesame seeds
- 80 g butter
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp orange zest and sugar
- pinch of salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line a tray.
2. Cream the butter and sugar, then add the egg.
3. Fold in the flour with the sesame seeds, the butter, and the flavoring.
4. Shape into small portions and bake until just golden at the edges.
5. Cool before storing or finishing with syrup or jam.

## Serving Note
Store in a tin and serve with coffee or tea.
