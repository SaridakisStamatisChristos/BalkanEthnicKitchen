# Zagreb Tomato Cucumber Salad

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style salad centers on tomatoes with cucumber, seasoned with onion, rosemary, and fresh cheese. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 400 g tomatoes
- 1 large cucumber
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of onion, rosemary, and fresh cheese
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the tomatoes and the cucumber into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
