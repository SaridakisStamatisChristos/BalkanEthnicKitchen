# Janjetina Ispod Peke

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Roast
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style roast centers on lamb with potatoes, seasoned with garlic, rosemary, and olive oil. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g lamb
- 400 g potatoes
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, rosemary, and olive oil
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the lamb lightly with the onion, then add the potatoes and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
