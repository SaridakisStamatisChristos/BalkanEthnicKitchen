# Rozata

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Custard dessert
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 40 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style custard dessert centers on milk with eggs, seasoned with caramel, vanilla, and citrus. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 700 ml liquid
- 250 g or ml milk
- 4 large eggs
- 80 g sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp caramel, vanilla, and citrus

## Method
1. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the milk, the eggs, sugar, and butter.
3. Cook slowly, stirring often, until the pudding thickens and the flavors of caramel, vanilla, and citrus come through.
4. Spoon into bowls or a serving dish.
5. Serve warm or chilled.

## Serving Note
Serve warm for comfort or chilled for a firmer set.
