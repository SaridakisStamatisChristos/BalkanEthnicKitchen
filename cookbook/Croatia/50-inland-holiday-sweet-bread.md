# Inland Holiday Sweet Bread

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Sweet bread
- Yield: 1 loaf
- Time: 2 hours 30 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style sweet bread centers on almond with milk, seasoned with citrus zest, vanilla, and butter. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 450 g flour
- 7 g yeast
- 120 ml warm liquid
- 80 g sugar
- 2 eggs
- 80 g butter
- 80 g almond
- 250 g or ml milk
- 1 tbsp citrus zest, vanilla, and butter

## Method
1. Mix the dough and knead until smooth, then let it rise until doubled.
2. Work in the almond, the milk, and the flavoring.
3. Shape into a braid or loaf and let it rise again.
4. Brush with egg and bake until deeply golden.
5. Cool before slicing so the crumb sets properly.

## Serving Note
Slice once cool and serve plain or with extra jam.
