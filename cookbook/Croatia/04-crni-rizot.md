# Crni Rizot

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Rice main
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style rice main centers on squid with rice, seasoned with garlic, wine, and parsley. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 600 g squid
- 180 g rice
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of garlic, wine, and parsley
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the fat in a wide pan and soften the onion.
2. Add the squid and the rice, and cook until the mixture starts to concentrate.
3. Season with garlic, wine, and parsley and cook until the pan looks glossy and thick.
4. If eggs or cheese belong in the dish, fold them through right at the end.
5. Serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Serving Note
Bring the whole pan to the table with bread for scooping.
