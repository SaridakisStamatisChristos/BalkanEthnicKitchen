# Strukli

- Country: Croatia
- Category: Rolled pastry
- Yield: 8 to 10 slices
- Time: 2 hours

## Intro
This original Croatia home-style rolled pastry centers on fresh cheese with cream, seasoned with butter and nutmeg. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, Adriatic fish, chard, cured meats, and slow braises.

## Ingredients
- 350 g flour
- 7 g yeast
- 150 ml warm milk or water
- 60 g butter
- 180 g fresh cheese
- 250 g or ml cream
- 1 egg for brushing
- 1 tbsp butter and nutmeg

## Method
1. Prepare the enriched dough and let it rise once.
2. Spread the filling made from the fresh cheese, the cream, and the flavoring across the dough.
3. Roll tightly, place in the pan, and let it proof again.
4. Bake until golden and fragrant.
5. Cool enough to slice cleanly.

## Serving Note
Slice after a short cooling period so the swirl holds.
