# Byrek me Spinaq

- Country: Albania
- Category: Savory pie
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Albania home-style savory pie centers on spinach with brined cheese, seasoned with dill and yogurt. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, brined cheese, yogurt, mint, peppers, and lamb.

## Ingredients
- 8 sheets phyllo or 500 g thin pie dough
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 300 g spinach
- 180 g brined cheese
- 2 eggs
- 200 g yogurt
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of dill and yogurt
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 C and oil a baking tray.
2. Cook the onion with the spinach until softened, then fold in the brined cheese and the dill and yogurt.
3. Whisk the eggs and yogurt with a pinch of salt to make a loose binding mixture.
4. Layer the dough with oil, spoon the filling between layers, and finish with the yogurt mixture over the top.
5. Bake until bronzed and crisp, then rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Serving Note
Serve warm with a spoon of yogurt and wedges of lemon.
