# Tave Kosi

- Country: Albania
- Category: Baked main
- Yield: 6 servings
- Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Intro
This original Albania home-style baked main centers on lamb with rice, seasoned with yogurt, mint, and black pepper. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, brined cheese, yogurt, mint, peppers, and lamb.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g lamb
- 180 g rice
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 250 g tomato or yogurt sauce
- 250 ml stock or water
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of yogurt, mint, and black pepper
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Brown the lamb lightly with the onion, then add the rice and the seasoning blend.
3. Transfer everything to a baking dish, moisten with sauce and stock, and cover loosely.
4. Bake until the tray is bubbling and the top starts to bronze, uncovering near the end.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Note
Serve after a short rest so the tray sets and the juices settle.
