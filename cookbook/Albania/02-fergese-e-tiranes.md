# Fergese e Tiranes

- Country: Albania
- Category: Skillet main
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 35 minutes

## Intro
This original Albania home-style skillet main centers on peppers with brined cheese, seasoned with tomato, paprika, and garlic. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, brined cheese, yogurt, mint, peppers, and lamb.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 6 medium peppers
- 160 g brined cheese
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp seasoning blend of tomato, paprika, and garlic
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the fat in a wide pan and soften the onion.
2. Add the peppers and the brined cheese, and cook until the mixture starts to concentrate.
3. Season with tomato, paprika, and garlic and cook until the pan looks glossy and thick.
4. If eggs or cheese belong in the dish, fold them through right at the end.
5. Serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Serving Note
Bring the whole pan to the table with bread for scooping.
