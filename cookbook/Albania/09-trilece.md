# Trilece

- Country: Albania
- Category: Milk cake
- Yield: 8 slices
- Time: 1 hour

## Intro
This original Albania home-style milk cake centers on milk with cream, seasoned with caramel and vanilla. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, brined cheese, yogurt, mint, peppers, and lamb.

## Ingredients
- 220 g flour or semolina
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 120 ml neutral liquid
- 250 g or ml milk
- 250 g or ml cream
- 80 g melted butter or oil
- 1 tbsp caramel and vanilla
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 C and line or grease a tin.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs, then add the liquid and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour, the milk, the cream, and the flavoring.
4. Spread into the tin and bake until the center springs back.
5. Cool slightly before slicing, adding syrup if the cake style calls for it.

## Serving Note
Serve with coffee, tea, or a small glass of dessert wine.
