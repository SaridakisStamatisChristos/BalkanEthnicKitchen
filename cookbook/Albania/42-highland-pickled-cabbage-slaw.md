# Highland Pickled Cabbage Slaw

- Country: Albania
- Category: Salad
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 20 minutes

## Intro
This original Albania home-style salad centers on cabbage with carrot, seasoned with vinegar, pepper, and dill. It is written for this collection and inspired by the country's broader pantry of olive oil, brined cheese, yogurt, mint, peppers, and lamb.

## Ingredients
- 300 g cabbage
- 200 g carrot
- 1 small onion or handful of olives
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 tbsp chopped seasoning mix of vinegar, pepper, and dill
- salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cut the cabbage and the carrot into bite-size pieces.
2. Add onion or olives if using.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon, and the seasoning mix.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay fresh and crisp.
5. Serve right away.

## Serving Note
Serve immediately while the vegetables are crisp.
